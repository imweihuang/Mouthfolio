# Original synthetic examples

`initial.json` and `corrected.json` were authored for this demo. Every company,
voice, value, identifier and excerpt is fictional. These original files are
released under the repository's MIT license, independently of third-party source
or market-data rights. No retained filing, provider payload or customer record
was used to populate them.

The first edition becomes available on October 26, 2026 at 12:00 UTC. A correction
becomes available a day later. The earlier edition remains immutable. Customer
retention is deliberately absent to test missing-data behavior. The UI always
labels the example synthetic; this is not investment research about a real entity.
